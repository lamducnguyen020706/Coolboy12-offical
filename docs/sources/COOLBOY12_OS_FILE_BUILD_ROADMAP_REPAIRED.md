## 0.5 The granularity rule — declared once, applied everywhere

> **RULE G.** A **specification** and a **schema** are always separate artifacts. A specification is model-owned architecture changing at Kind-admission ceremony; a schema is the Registry-governed structural contract changing at field-definition revision. Different owners, different lifecycles, different validation → **two artifacts, always.**
>
> **RULE G2.** A model's **worked examples** and its **tests** are always separate artifacts. Examples prove architecture is *usable*; tests prove it is *enforced*.
>
> **RULE G3.** Files sharing one responsibility, one lifecycle, one owner and one validation are **one artifact** even if they are many files — declared explicitly at the entry (adapter shells, refusing commands).

Applied to all 49 Kinds: 98 Kind artifacts, no exceptions. This adds 20 artifacts against the prior roadmap and removes its inconsistency.
